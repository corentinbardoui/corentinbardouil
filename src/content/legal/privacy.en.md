# Privacy policy

Last updated: {{date}}

This page explains what data is collected on corentinbardouil.com, what it is used for, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights.

## Data controller

Corentin Bardouil, sole proprietor (entrepreneur individuel), 4 rue Louis Guilloux, 56890 Saint-Avé, France. Contact: corentin.bardouil@gmail.com.

## The data collected and its uses

**A “For your company” request.** Your name, your company, your work email and, if you provide them, your phone number and your topic. They are only used to get back to you about your request. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract. These requests are handled by Corentin Bardouil and his partner.

**Booking a 1:1 session.** Your name, your email, your company, your billing details, the topic of the session and any documents you choose to share. They are used to organize the session, invoice it and send you the written summary. Legal basis: performance of the contract; invoicing is also a legal obligation. Payment is processed by Stripe: neither the site nor Corentin Bardouil sees or stores your card details.

**Your emails.** The messages you send are used to reply to you.

**Audience measurement.** The site uses no audience measurement tool.

## Recipients and service providers

Your data is never sold or rented. It is processed by Corentin Bardouil and, for company requests, by his partner. The following providers process it on their behalf: Vercel for hosting, Formspree for receiving requests, Cal.com for booking, Stripe for payment and Google (Gmail) for email.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, in particular in the United States. These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided for by the GDPR: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or standard contractual clauses.

## Retention periods

- Company requests and emails: 3 years after the last exchange.
- Bookings: 3 years after the session.
- Invoices and accounting records: 10 years, as required by French law.

## Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, ask for it to be deleted, object to its processing, restrict its use or ask for its portability by writing to corentin.bardouil@gmail.com. You will receive an answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

## Cookies

The site sets no advertising cookies. Only the cookies needed for the booking calendar and payment are used.
